# Case Study: Corporate Insolvency - Know Your Customer -  Hidden Signals of exposure to a Corporate Insolvency

How did this happen? 
In January 2018, the Carillion group appeared to be a substantial and successful business, with a strong presence in the construction and facilities management sectors. However, behind the scenes, the company was struggling with mounting debts and financial mismanagement. The company's leadership failed to address these issues effectively, leading to a loss of confidence among investors and stakeholders. Within months, its companies were placed into compulsory liquidation, resulting in significant job losses and financial repercussions for creditors and suppliers. 

The collapse of Carillion serves as a cautionary tale for businesses and investors, highlighting the importance of transparency, effective governance, and proactive risk management in corporate operations. 

This demo walks through the timeline of the corporate collapse as evidenced by Companies House data and The Gazette notices, and then re-runs events from the perspective of a subcontractor, looking at how common KYC practices could have easily failed to identify the company's exposure to risk through events in the Carillion group. 

Finally, the scenario is rerun with the application of rules and reasoning in RDFox over Companies House Persons with Significant Control (PSC) data and The Gazette insolvency notices to demonstrate how RDFox could be used to alert a company early on, to indirect events that could indicate risk exposure. 

Through the use of RDFox, this demo illustrates more generally how a knowledge graph can be combined with rules and queries to infer and analyse the ownership structures and relationships of companies, to gain insights into the financial health and stability of a company's business partners, suppliers, and clients.
